Prices are fetched by a scheduled job and committed to this site, not called live from your browser. Data updates roughly every 30 minutes.
"7d change" is poe.ninja's own week-over-week trend. A ⚠ flag means very low trade volume, treat that price as unreliable, it can be skewed by a single odd listing.
Big Movers excludes thin-market currencies and anything worth under 1 chaos (tiny-value items can swing wildly in % terms without meaning anything), and splits results into Rising (consider selling) and Dropping (maybe a cheap buy).
Currency count varies slightly run to run: poe.ninja only lists currencies with at least one active listing at snapshot time, we don't cut the list down ourselves.
